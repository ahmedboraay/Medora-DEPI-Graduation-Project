MEDORA – DEPI Graduation Project Defense Preparation Plan

1.1 Project overview
Medora is a proposed patient-first digital healthcare platform with two interfaces: a patient experience for organizing medical records and care tasks, and a doctor experience for viewing records that the patient has authorized.
Project promise: “Medora brings a patient’s medical documents and care tasks into one organized timeline, so patients can retrieve information and share selected records with authorized doctors more easily. AI supports organization and summarization; clinical decisions remain with qualified healthcare professionals.”
1.2 Problem and hypothesis
Problem framing: important health information can be fragmented across paper files, messaging apps, lab portals, hospitals, email and personal devices. This can make it harder for patients to retrieve relevant records and prepare for consultations.
Hypothesis—not a proven market finding: a patient-controlled timeline, consent-based sharing and follow-up reminders may reduce the effort needed to find and share relevant information. Validate through patient and clinician research.

1.3 Proposed user journey
1.	Patient creates a profile and opens their personal record space.
2.	Patient uploads or adds a medical document, report, scan, prescription or care task.
3.	The system organizes records by type/date/source; AI may assist with classification or a draft summary.
4.	Patient reviews, corrects and confirms any AI-assisted information.
5.	Patient selects specific records and authorizes a doctor to view them.
6.	Doctor sees only authorized records and relevant context.
7.	Doctor view checks authorization and displays only selected/authorized records.
